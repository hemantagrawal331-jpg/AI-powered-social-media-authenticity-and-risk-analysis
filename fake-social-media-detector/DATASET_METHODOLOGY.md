# Dataset methodology (synthetic-v2)

FakeGuard does **not** ship live Instagram/X dumps. Training data is generated by
`backend/app/ml/dataset.py` with a fixed seed.

## Why synthetic, and why overlapping

A cartoon generator (`low followers + random username = fake`) produces a separable toy problem
and a 100% holdout score that means nothing. v2 samples from class-conditional distributions with
shared support:

- Genuine businesses may post on a schedule (high periodicity).
- Genuine creators use promotional links and hashtags.
- Sophisticated bots have human-looking bios (adversarial / LLM-style text) while keeping bot-like
  graph and cadence.
- Coordinated accounts can look ordinary in isolation.
- Compromised-looking accounts keep a long genuine history and then shift language.

## Archetypes and approximate mix

| Archetype | Share | Binary label |
|-----------|-------|----------------|
| Genuine personal | 28% | 0 |
| Genuine creator | 14% | 0 |
| Genuine business | 10% | 0 |
| Spam | 12% | 1 |
| Basic bot | 8% | 1 |
| Sophisticated bot | 8% | 1 (held out of training) |
| Coordinated | 8% | 1 |
| Impersonation | 6% | 1 |
| Compromised-looking | 3% | 1 |
| Unknown / ambiguous | 3% | noisy |

About 4% of labels are flipped so the classes are not perfectly separable.

## Distributions (assumptions, not scraped moments)

- Followers: log-normal, parameters depend on archetype.
- Account age: gamma-like (`gammavariate`).
- Engagement rate: derived from likes/comments vs followers; fake classes mix ghost-low and
  sometimes inflated counts.
- Cadence: regular clock hours for bots/business schedulers; mixed human hours otherwise.
- Username: real-name style vs `user`+digits vs campaign templates.

Prevalence of inauthentic accounts on real platforms is often cited in a **3–20%** range and is
platform- and era-dependent. The Bayesian prior in config is 0.12 — an assumption, not a fact.

## Splits

Default generator size: **20,000** accounts (scalable). Training uses:

- 70% of non-held-out rows for fitting
- 15% validation (calibration)
- 15% test

Plus:

- **Archetype holdout:** `sophisticated_bot` never enters the training mass.
- **Adversarial ablation:** content-only RF vs metadata/behavior RF on that holdout.
- Fairness slices on the test set: new accounts, numeric usernames, high-frequency posters.

## External datasets

Adapters in `backend/app/ml/adapters.py` map TwiBot-style JSON, Cresci-style CSV, and generic CSV
into a canonical record **if you already have a license**. This repository does not redistribute
those corpora.

## Leakage controls

The live feature vector is extracted from `ProfileInput` fields only (`intelligence/vector.py`).
There is no “archetype” column at inference. Analyzer 0–100 heuristic scores are **not** the ML
features (they were in v1 and made the synthetic task trivially separable).
